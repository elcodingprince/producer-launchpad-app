Producer Launchpad v1 billing

Stripe recurring subscription tracking

The goal is to let the app know whether each Shopify store has an active recurring Stripe subscription. This controls merchant access to app tools like uploads, catalog setup, licenses, and delivery management.

This is not the full money system. The upfront setup fee remains a manual Stripe Invoice or Payment Link step before onboarding.

Scope

In scope

  • Store Stripe customer and subscription IDs by Shopify shop.
  • Read Stripe subscription status from webhooks.
  • Allow active and trialing stores into merchant app tools.
  • Keep past-due stores working while showing a warning.
  • Block inactive, canceled, unpaid, incomplete, or missing subscriptions.
  • Show billing status on Settings.
  • Open Stripe Customer Portal when a Stripe customer exists and portal is enabled in Stripe.

Out of scope

  • No Shopify Billing API.
  • No setup-fee checkout, invoice, or payment-link automation.
  • No CRM or pipeline automation.
  • No automatic file, storage, delivery, or customer portal cleanup.
  • No changes to licenses, downloads, checkout delivery, or theme code.
  • No broad self-serve onboarding.

Billing status policy

Stripe status Merchant access What the app says
active Full access The subscription is active.
trialing Full access The store is in a Stripe trial and sees the trial end date when available.
past_due Access stays on Payment needs attention while Stripe retries payment.
unpaid Blocked Paid merchant tools are paused until billing is fixed.
canceled Blocked The recurring subscription is canceled.
incomplete Blocked Checkout or payment setup still needs to be completed.
incomplete_expired Blocked The subscription setup expired before payment was completed.
missing Blocked No recurring Stripe subscription is connected to the shop.

Trials

Shopify install does not automatically start a trial. For founding members, a trial exists only when Stripe has a subscription with status trialing. When Stripe sends a trial end date, Settings shows that date.

Payment failure

When payment fails, Stripe usually moves the subscription to past_due first. Producer Launchpad keeps merchant access enabled during that state and shows a payment warning.

The app does not calculate its own grace period. Stripe dunning and retry settings decide when a subscription becomes unpaid or canceled.

Cancellation

If a subscription is set to cancel at period end, the store stays active until Stripe reports that the subscription is canceled. Once canceled, paid merchant app tools are blocked.

Existing customer download links stay available in v1. This task does not delete files, storage objects, delivery records, or prior customer access.

Deferred on purpose

  • Setup-fee automation stays manual through Stripe Invoice or Payment Link.
  • Storage cleanup and cancellation lifecycle rules are separate business-policy work.
  • Customer download portal routes remain available even if merchant billing becomes inactive.
  • Stripe Customer Portal setup is handled inside Stripe, not inside Producer Launchpad.
  • Plan upgrades, self-serve onboarding, and internal billing dashboards are not part of this v1 task.
